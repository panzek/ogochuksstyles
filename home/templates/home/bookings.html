Booking Appointments

Things to know
before your appointment

  • For a hair appointment , it's best to arrive with your hair dry and shampooed.
  • Don't use oil or hair products such as styling gels or mousses on the same day you want to get your hair done.
  • Come with your hair detangled. I must be able to comb through your hair freely. Failure for you to be compliant will result in you being sent home.
