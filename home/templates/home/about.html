Satin-Lined Skull Caps

Our Satin-Lined Skull Caps are carefully designed to provide extra protection for your hair with our satin lining. The satin material reduces friction, minimises breakage, retains hair's moisture, keeping it healthier and more manageable.

Our skull caps combine trending fashion designs with practicality. Ever have your hair ruined after a footbal session in the rain? Frustrated when your hair begins to frizz just days after styling? These cap let you look your best without compromising your haircare routine.

Perhaps you're tired of walking around in a traditional bonnet? These caps offer the perfect alternative for a polished, stylish appearance, whether you're training at Astro, running in Santry or heading out to meet some friends.

What makes our Caps different?

Each Satin-Lined Cap features an innovative waterproof material sewn into the design, offering convenience and durability. This essential layer reduces the risk of water soaking through to the inner satin lining, keeping your hair protected from excess moisture.
